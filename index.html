HyperGryph

A Shanghai game studio that built one hit — Arknights — into a whole apparatus: an animation studio, a music label, a merchandise arm, and a global publishing brand, all funded almost entirely off that one game's revenue. This page pulls together everything gathered while researching the company, Gravity Well Studio, and cinematic director Yun Li (Naomi V).

01

Company & founders

Founded January 24, 2017 by a small team that came out of Yostar, MICA Team, and the China Academy of Art. The company grew almost entirely off Arknights' own revenue rather than VC funding — its one disclosed outside investment came from Yao Meng, CEO of Yostar, its eventual global publisher.

Cover photo, GameLook conference writeup, Dec 2025

Huang Yifeng

Co-founder & CEO

Former Google programmer, Shanghai Jiao Tong University graduate. Active on China's game-culture conference circuit — talks about using Arknights to modernize the presentation of Chinese culture through immersive exhibitions and brand crossovers (Shanghai Metro livery, White Rabbit candy).

Meeting Hideo Kojima, Shanghai, Oct 2025 (Gamersky)

Zhong Qixiang

"Hai Mao" · Co-founder, VP & Head Producer

B. 1991, China Academy of Art architecture graduate. Art director on Girls' Frontline before co-founding HyperGryph; calls his design approach "functionalism" borrowed from architecture. Won the Japan Game Awards' Future Anticipation Award (Sept 2024) for Endfield.

02

Games & internal studios

Each release runs through its own differently-named internal studio — a structure that keeps growing as the company diversifies past its first hit.

Studio Montagne

Arknights (2019) — the flagship tower-defense/tactical RPG; still the main revenue driver.

Phonemix / Huánxíng Mountain

Arknights: Endfield — 3D real-time RPG spin-off, released Jan 22, 2026.

Awakening Wave / Nous Wave

Ex Astris — premium 3D RPG, launched Feb 2024; reportedly underperformed at its ¥68 price point.

Sùhuí / Suhuai Studio

POPUCOM — co-op puzzle-adventure, released mid-2025.

Gravity Well Studio

In-house animation — the promotional PV shorts covered in the next section.

GRYPHLINE

Global publishing brand, operated via Singapore entity Gryph Frontier Pte. Ltd.
